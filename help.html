Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a flight?

Choose your departure city, destination, travel dates, and then select the available flight that works best for you.

Can I change my travel date after booking?

Yes. You can request a date change through My Trips. Fare differences and change fees may apply depending on your ticket type.

How do refunds and cancellations work?

If your flight is cancelled by the airline, you can request a refund. Voluntary cancellations depend on the fare rules for your ticket.

How much baggage can I bring?

Cabin and checked baggage allowances depend on your route and ticket. You can add more baggage before checkout or later through My Trips.

When should I arrive at the airport?

Arrive at least 2 hours before domestic flights and 3 hours before international flights so you have enough time for check-in and security.

How do I check in online?

Online check-in opens before departure. Use your booking details in My Trips to confirm passenger information and download your boarding pass.
